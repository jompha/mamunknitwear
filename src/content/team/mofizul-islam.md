As one of the leading exporters of garments backed by a well-established buying house, we are planning to increase our share in the marketing of readymade garments from Bangladesh with the addition of new buyers to the list of customers, with penetration into the markets of CIS countries as well as Japan and other new markets – towards increasing our export volume and providing more and more orders to our existing as well as prospective factories – and thus contribute significantly in the earning of valuable foreign exchange for the country.

At the same time, we have planned to create more jobs with the establishment of modern compliance factories in garments and allied sectors and thus contribute significantly in the earning of foreign exchange by direct export of high quality products, with the addition of more value to the end products through the establishment of more backward linkage industries as well as adding more automatic Jacquard machines.

Keeping environmental and decent labor practice issues in mind, all our factories have been designed in a manner that satisfies both local and international legal requirements, which are necessary for responsible manufacturing.
