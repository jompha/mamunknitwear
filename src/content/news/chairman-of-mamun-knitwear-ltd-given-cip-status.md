In recognizing the contributions of the RMG sector to the national economy and creating jobs, the Bangladesh government has recently awarded **Md. Mofizul Islam**, Chairman, Mamun Knitwear Ltd. and Managing Director, M. M. Knitwear Ltd., with the status of commercially important person (CIP) Export for the year of 2017.

The CIP card is a recognition to the business people who contributed a lot to the Bangladesh economy by investing money in industrialization and created jobs for the countrymen.
